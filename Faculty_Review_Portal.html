🤖 4. Cattle Health Predictor (Predictive ML Model)

What We Did:

We built a complex dual-inference XGBoost backend engine (disease_model.pkl and milk_yield_model.pkl). It takes live vitals from the user and simultaneously predicts the probability of disease AND calculates the resulting negative impact on milk yield in real-time.

🎯 Optimum Values for Testing

  • Body Temp: Healthy is 38.5 - 39.2 °C. (Type >39.6 to force a fever alert)
  • Heart Rate: Healthy is 48 - 84 bpm.
  • SCC (Somatic Cell Count): Healthy is < 200,000. (Type 300000+ to simulate infection)
  • Resting Hours: Healthy is 10 - 14 hrs. (Type <8 to simulate lameness)

How to Predict (Step-by-Step Guide):

  1. Go to the "Cattle Health Predictor" tab in the dashboard.
  2. Sub-section (Input Vitals): Using the optimum values above, type your desired numbers into the 4 input fields (Temp, Heart Rate, SCC, Rest).
  3. Click the "Run Dual AI Diagnosis" button.
  4. Result: Observe the two output gauges. The left gauge shows the % Risk of Disease. The right gauge calculates the Expected Yield Drop in Liters based on the severity of the vitals.

📈 5. Milk Yield Forecaster (Predictive ML Model)

What We Did:

We trained an XGBoost regression model on historical parity data (processed_lactations.csv). It forecasts a cow's absolute peak yield and total 305-day lactation volume based entirely on her previous historical cycles and rest periods.

🎯 Optimum Values for Testing

  • Previous Lactation Yield: Standard Holstein yield is 8,000 - 12,000 kg.
  • Lactation Number (Parity): Peak production occurs between parity 3 and 5.
  • Days Dry: The biological optimum is 45 - 60 days of rest.

How to Predict (Step-by-Step Guide):

  1. Sub-section (Genetics & History): Enter the cow's 'Previous Lactation Yield' (e.g., 9000 kg).
  2. Enter her 'Age' and 'Lactation Number' (Parity).
  3. Enter 'Days Dry' (e.g., 55 days).
  4. Click the "Forecast Next Lactation" button.
  5. Result: View the AI projection cards detailing the precise Expected Peak Yield (kg/day) and Total Volume (kg/yr).

🩺 7. Mastitis Early Warning (Predictive ML Model)

What We Did:

We engineered critical features from the clinical_mastitis_cows.csv dataset and trained an XGBoost classifier. This model detects sub-clinical mastitis by analyzing IoT flex sensors that measure minute udder swelling (ADC expansion ratios during inhale/exhale cycles) paired with core temperature.

🎯 Optimum Values for Testing

  • Core Temperature: Enter > 39.5 °C to simulate severe fever.
  • Avg Flex Swelling (ADC): Normal expansion is ~200. Values > 300 indicate abnormal swelling (inflammation).

How to Predict (Step-by-Step Guide):

  1. Sub-section (Sensor Data): Enter the Core Temp (e.g., 39.8 °C).
  2. Enter the Avg Flex Swelling (e.g., 320 for severe swelling).
  3. Use the dropdowns to toggle Hardness/Pain symptoms.
  4. Click the "Run XGBoost Diagnostic" button.
  5. Result: The AI will calculate the exact probability % that the cow currently has clinical mastitis.
