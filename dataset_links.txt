Ag-AI Dairy Farming Datasets
===============================

1. Global Cattle Disease Dataset (250k rows)
   Source: Kaggle (Cattle Health and Feeding Records)
   Usage: Multi-class symptom diagnosis, Dual AI Simulator (Health)

2. Global Cattle Milk Yield Prediction Dataset
   Source: Kaggle
   Usage: Dual AI Simulator (Yield prediction based on vitals/environment)

3. Superior Dairy Sires Records (Lactation Curves)
   Source: Mendeley Data (Long-term lactation records)
   Usage: Peak Yield Predictor (4-Year research tab)

4. Czech Republic Milk Quality & Genetics Dataset
   Source: Mendeley Data (Association between production and reproduction parameters)
   Usage: Milk Quality Predictor (Fat/Protein Component AI)

5. MmCows: Multimodal Dairy Cattle Dataset
   Source: Kaggle / Hugging Face
   Usage: Simulated IoT and behavioral analytics data

6. Cow Mastitis & Visual Datasets
   Source: Kaggle / Clinical Mastitis Cows v2
   Usage: IoT Mastitis Warning Sensor Model, CNN Vision Mockup Model
